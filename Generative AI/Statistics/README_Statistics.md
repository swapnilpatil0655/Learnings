<h2>📚 Important Statistical Concepts</h2>

<h3>1️⃣ Mean</h3>

<div style="background-color:#f0fdf4;padding:15px;border-radius:10px;">

<p><b>Definition:</b> Average value of the data.</p>

<h4>Formula:</h4>

<pre>
Mean = Sum of Observations / Total Observations
</pre>

<p><b>Purpose:</b> Represents central tendency of data.</p>

</div>

<br>

<h3>2️⃣ Median</h3>

<div style="background-color:#fff7ed;padding:15px;border-radius:10px;">

<p><b>Definition:</b> Middle value of sorted data.</p>

<p><b>Purpose:</b> Useful when data contains outliers.</p>

</div>

<h2>📊 Data Visualization</h2>

<h3>1️⃣ Histogram</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eef2ff;padding:15px;border-radius:10px;">

<p>
A histogram uses vertical bars to represent frequency distribution.
</p>

<p>
✔️ Helps understand data spread and patterns.
</p>

</div>

<br>

<h3>2️⃣ PDF (Probability Density Function)</h3>

<div style="background-color:#ecfeff;padding:15px;border-radius:10px;">

<p>
A smooth probability distribution curve derived from histogram data.
</p>

</div>

<br>

<h3>3️⃣ CDF (Cumulative Density Function)</h3>

<div style="background-color:#fdf2f8;padding:15px;border-radius:10px;">

<p>
Represents cumulative probability up to a specific value.
</p>

</div>
